Euro Super Monte Carlo - official currency and short-rate inputs

1. EUR/USD exchange rate
Source: European Central Bank (ECB) Data Portal
Series: EXR.M.USD.EUR.SP00.A
Definition: Monthly average US dollars per euro reference exchange rate.

2. Euro short-term rate before October 2019
Source: European Central Bank (ECB) Data Portal
Series: RTD.M.S0.N.C_EONIA.E
Definition: Monthly historical EONIA rate.

3. Euro short-term rate from October 2019
Source: European Central Bank (ECB) Data Portal
Series: EST.B.EU000A2X2A25.WT
Definition: Euro short-term rate (€STR), daily business-week observations; model converts these to monthly averages.

4. US short-term rate
Source: Federal Reserve / FRED
Series: DFF
Definition: Effective Federal Funds Rate, daily; model converts these to monthly averages.

Local-cache method
The application attempts to download these fixed official series once and saves cleaned monthly CSV files in a folder named local_macro_data beside the Python scripts. Subsequent launches use those local files and do not require a fresh download. The Refresh button explicitly requests new official observations.

Historical FX conversion for USD-quoted supported proxies:
1 + R_EUR = (1 + R_USD) * (FX_(t-1) / FX_t)
where FX is USD per EUR.

Approximate hedged component:
R_hedged ≈ R_USD + (r_EUR - r_USD) / 12

Partial hedge ratio h:
R_final = (1-h) * R_unhedged + h * R_hedged
